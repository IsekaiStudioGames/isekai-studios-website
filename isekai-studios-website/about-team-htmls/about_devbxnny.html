Hi, I’m devbxnny. I'm a game developer focused on AI behavior, UI, and weird little interactive experiences with too much personality.

🎮 What I Do

I build gameplay features, enemy behaviors, player systems, and UI interactions. I like making mechanics feel readable, reactive, and fun to mess with.

🧠 My Focus

My favorite work is AI systems. Enemies that chase, react, attack, drop items, and make the player go “oh no, it has thoughts.”

🌙 My Style

I love games with strong atmosphere, charming characters, horror elements, strange worlds, and a little bit of inventory management sprinkled in for flavor.

🐇 Current Quest

Currently working on the Isekai Studios website, including page layout, visual polish, interactive navigation, team/member pages, and showcasing upcoming studio projects.

About Me

Outside of development, I’m usually playing horror games or games where there is at least one character I can get way too attached to.

I also love Vocaloid, metal music, drawing, and taking care of my gargoyle gecko, Alphys, my tiny lizard creature who deserves her own side quest.

One of my biggest creative inspirations is Toby Fox. I love the way his games mix humor, emotion, music, strange characters, and unforgettable story moments into something that feels deeply personal and completely unique.
