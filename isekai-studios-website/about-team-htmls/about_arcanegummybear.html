Jared Hadaway

A stylized environment artist focused on creating memorable worlds through expressive design, creative texturing, and a blend of realistic and stylized art.

🎨 What I Do

I create stylized environment art and explore other parts of the development pipeline, including visual effects and character design. My work focuses on crafting the pieces that help new worlds feel complete, distinctive, and alive.

🌿 My Focus

Stylized environment art is my main focus as an artist. I love exploring other parts of the pipeline like VFX and character design, but environment art is my home and specialty.

✨ My Style

Stylized art is my passion, but I also enjoy working with a blend of realistic and stylized direction. I love adding extra creativity to my texturing, giving assets a unique edge that I feel is often missing from purely realistic art.

⚔️ Current Quest

Currently, I’m working on Hollowed Shard, a project with a partner studio. I’m also taking part in a couple of mentorships to continue advancing my skills as an artist.

A Little About Me

I’m kind of obsessive about improving my skills and learning from my peers in the industry, so my life is consumed with 3D and creative pursuits. Spending time on my computer crafting the pieces needed to construct a new world is one of my happiest places.

I draw a lot of inspiration from fantasy shows and films, especially Tolkien and Studio Ghibli. I’m always daydreaming about my own stories and worlds to bring to life.

My favorite games are, unsurprisingly, RPGs and narrative-driven or story-focused games. While I’m working, I’m usually listening to techno, house, trance, or old-school R&B so I can relax and space out.
