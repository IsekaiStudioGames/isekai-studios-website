A multidisciplinary developer and creative lead helping shape Isekai Studios through programming, art, production support, and the behind-the-scenes problem-solving that keeps ambitious ideas moving forward.

🛠️ What I Do

I wear a lot of hats at Isekai Studios. My work spans programming, reviewing and approving design documents, researching tools and resources, organizing development priorities, and stepping in wherever the studio needs support.

A lot of what I do is about keeping the larger vision moving: connecting ideas, solving roadblocks, and making sure the team has what it needs to turn concepts into something real.

💻 My Focus

My main areas of focus are programming and art. I enjoy moving between the technical and creative sides of development, whether that means building systems, refining visual ideas, or helping shape the direction of a project.

I am most comfortable when I can combine logic, experimentation, and creativity rather than staying confined to a single role.

🎨 My Style

My style is adaptable, experimental, and driven by the needs of each project. I like work that feels expressive without losing structure, and technical systems that still leave room for personality and creative risk.

I tend to gravitate toward ideas that are a little chaotic, unconventional, or difficult to define at first—the kind that become stronger through iteration and collaboration.

⚔️ Current Quest

My current quest is helping finalize and strengthen Isekai Studios alongside the rest of the team. That means improving the studio's foundation, tightening our creative direction, and preparing us for larger projects ahead.

The goal is to build something sustainable, recognizable, and capable of growing far beyond a single project.

A Little About Me

I become deeply invested in the things I create. Once a project has my attention, I tend to throw myself into it completely, sometimes to the point where work-life balance becomes more of an optional side quest.

I thrive in creative chaos—the process of taking scattered ideas, half-formed concepts, and technical problems and slowly shaping them into something cohesive. Building, experimenting, and watching a world come together piece by piece is where I feel most at home.

I am always looking for ways to improve the studio, strengthen the team, and push our projects closer to the vision we started with.
