📜 The Legend of Isekai Studios

In the beginning... there were five developers who dreamed of something bigger. Inspired by isekai stories—where ordinary people are transported to fantastical worlds—they asked: "What if we could create those portals?"

The Vision: Games should feel like stepping into another world. Not just playing a character, but truly being transported somewhere new. Where magic is real, choices have weight, and every adventure feels personal.

The Philosophy: We believe in handcrafted experiences over formulaic templates. In meaningful stories over mindless grinding. In games that respect your time while offering endless depth.

The Journey: Like any good isekai protagonist, we're leveling up with each project, learning new skills, and growing stronger. Our first game is just the beginning of an epic saga.

🌟 "Every master was once a beginner. Every legend started with a single quest."
