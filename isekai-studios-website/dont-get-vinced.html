📖 About the Quest

Don’t Get Vinced is a fast-paced team project set in Rockstar City, Chicago, 1999. After crime boss Vince “The Viper” Malone steals a cloning virus and unleashes an army of himself. Doug, a chili dog-loving brawler, must fight through the chaos and save the city.

📜 The Story

Rockstar City, Chicago, 1999. It is a playground for wannabe stars and criminals trying to make it big. When crime boss Vince “The Viper” Malone steals a top-secret cloning virus, he creates an army of himself and sets his sights on taking over the city.

Now it is up to Doug to fight back, stop the invasion, and teach Vince a lesson he will never forget.
