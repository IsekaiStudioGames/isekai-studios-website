📖 About the Quest

May All Your Bacon Burn is the debut title from Isekai Studios, a unique adventure that blends magic, mystery, and culinary chaos. Step into a world where cooking isn't just a skill—it's survival, and every flame tells a story.

Navigate through enchanted kitchens, face mischievous fire spirits, and uncover the secrets behind an ancient curse. With innovative gameplay mechanics and a captivating narrative, this game puts a fresh spin on the adventure genre.

📜 The Story

In a realm where cooking and magic are one, a terrible curse has been unleashed: all bacon shall burn. What begins as a culinary inconvenience quickly spirals into an adventure across enchanted kitchens and mystical realms.

As you delve deeper into the mystery, you'll discover that this curse is just the beginning. Ancient powers stir, long-forgotten recipes hold the key to salvation, and someone—or something—is manipulating events from the shadows. Can you lift the curse before all hope turns to ash?
